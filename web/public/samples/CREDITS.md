# Sample photo credits

Both are real photographs, bundled so the photo flow can be tried on a machine with no camera.
Reports made from them are stored as synthetic, because nobody was standing at the reported spot.

  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anti-slip_Technology_on_a_Curb_Cut.jpg (resized). It is in the classifier's training split.
- `faces.jpg`: the Apollo 11 crew portrait, NASA, public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apollo_11_Crew.jpg (resized). Not a curb: it is here to show the face blur.
